Signal Station: free IB study games and a chess lab

Signal Station is a free, no-sign-up site of study games for IB Diploma students and chess players. Scores are saved only in your browser.

Quiz Arcade

Multiple-choice quiz decks for IB Economics, IB Psychology and IB Biology, plus Sport Lab and Game Theory decks. Each answer comes with a short explanation, and new questions are added every week.

Chess Lab

A daily chess puzzle and a library of classic checkmate patterns, including the back-rank mate, Arabian mate, corner knight and queen box. Includes a puzzle builder for setting your own positions.

Club Deck

A presentation builder for school chess clubs: build puzzle slides and export them to PowerPoint in one click.

Link-4

A daily word-connection grid: find the four groups of four linked words.

AI Lab

Short interactive missions that explain how AI and ratings work with sliders and charts instead of code, starting with the Elo rating formula used in chess. New missions unlock on Saturdays.
